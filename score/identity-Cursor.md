# Study identity

Container lengths, tempos, and collections were fixed before note generation. Every study is 8 bars. No stochastic draws.

## s01 Paper Window

Variation A sits on beat 1. The first chord is four notes, then three. Bars 4 to 6 shorten, and bar 7 holds. Variation B starts on beat 2, uses a different inversion, and lasts one beat.

Limitations: Pair keys A with answer A, and keys B with answer B. The answer is a reference file. It is not inside the keys file.

## s02 Late Seventh

Variation A enters on the and of 1 and holds for two beats, longer on the last bar. Variation B is on the downbeat, a dotted eighth, with the seventh on top.

Limitations: The answer plays only four notes in A and two in B. Silence is the rest of the line.

## s03 Open Fifths

Variation A is two-note fifths on even bars, held almost the whole bar, with odd bars empty. Variation B is a three-note chord on beat 3 of every bar, one eighth long.

Limitations: Odd bars of keys A are intentional silence, not a missing file. The answer does not fill those bars in variation A.

## s04 Minor Ninth

Variation A starts on beat 2 with a three-note minor color and one flagged C#4. Variation B starts on beat 4, two notes, shorter, and never leaves the minor collection.

Limitations: The C# is one chord tone in keys A. It is not a new key, and it is not in the answer.

## s05 Suspended Room

Variation A arrives on beat 3 and lets the third in at bar 4. Variation B is a two-note sus shell on the downbeat and never states the third.

Limitations: C# in variation A is inside A mixolydian. It is not a departure. Variation B has no C#.

## s06 Low Register

Variation A sits low, on the and of 2, for under two beats. Variation B starts on beat 1 in a higher inversion and holds for most of the bar.

Limitations: This is the slowest study, 8 bars at 60 BPM, 32 seconds. It is not a 16-bar file.

## s07 Two-Note Shell

Variation A is two notes on beat 4, an eighth long. Variation B is three notes a 16th after the downbeat, held much longer, with a longer last bar.

Limitations: Beat 4 in variation A leaves the rest of the bar empty on purpose.

## s08 Cadence Hold

Variation A attacks twice in every bar: a short chord on beat 1 and a two-note hold on beat 3. Variation B is one chord every two bars, held for 7200 ticks. D# is in the collection and is not used.

Limitations: Variation B does not play on odd bars. That rest is written. Do not copy variation A into those bars.
